Part A — The W-4 (how much federal tax is withheld)

Too little withheld = a bill in April. Too much = a yearlong free loan to the government. Sane beats surprise on both ends.
Common settling-in slip-ups: claiming “exempt” when you're not; wrong Step-2 choice in a two-job household; forgetting to update after marriage, a new kid, or a side gig.

Part B — Direct deposit (the money actually reaching you)

One flipped digit and paycheck #1 becomes a two-week scavenger hunt.
