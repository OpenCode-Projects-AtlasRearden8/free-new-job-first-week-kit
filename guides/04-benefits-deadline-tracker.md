# Benefits Deadline Mini-Tracker

*Benefits enrollment windows are real deadlines with real money attached. Miss them and you may wait a full year for another shot. This mini-tracker keeps the dates in one page you can see weekly.*

From **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**. Local, printable, no data collection — your deadlines are nobody's business but yours.

## Step 1: Get the dates in writing

Ask HR (email works — you'll have a paper trail):

> "Could you confirm the benefits enrollment window and deadline for new hires, and where the enrollment portal is?"

## Step 3: Each decision, one question per row

You don't need to study benefits for a week (that deeper guide sums things up separately). One decision at a time:

- Medical: "What did I pay out of pocket last year, and which plan would have been cheapest at that usage?"
- Retirement: "At minimum, do I contribute at least the employer's full match?" (free money; leave it on the table only with a stated reason)
- HSA/FSA: "Any predictable health costs next year?" (FSA use-it-or-lose-it; HSA rolls over)
- Life/disability: "Would my household be okay if my paycheck paused?"

## Step 4: evidenced finish line

- [ ] All elections submitted, screenshot/confirmation saved
- [ ] Beneficiary names filled on **all** forms (the default is "estate," which is almost never what you want)
- [ ] Enrollment confirmation filed (see Papers: Keep or Shred)
- [ ] First-paycheck check: deductions match what you elected (see W-4 & Direct Deposit checklist)
