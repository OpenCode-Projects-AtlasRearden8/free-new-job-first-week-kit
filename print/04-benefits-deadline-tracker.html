Real deadlines, real money, one page  •  employer:

Step 1 — Get the dates in writing

Email HR: “Could you confirm the benefits enrollment window and deadline for new hires, and where the enrollment portal is?” Emails beat blurry memories — get the date, the time zone, and the year.

Step 3 — One question per decision

  • Medical: “What did I pay out of pocket last year — and which plan would have been cheapest at that usage?”
  • Retirement: “Do I contribute at least the full employer match?” (free money; leaving it takes a stated reason)
  • FSA vs HSA: FSA is use-it-or-lose-it; HSA rolls over. Predictable costs? Elect for them.
  • Life / disability: “Would my household be okay if my paycheck paused?”
