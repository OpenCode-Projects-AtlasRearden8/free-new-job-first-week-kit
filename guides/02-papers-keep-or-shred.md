# Papers: Keep or Shred

*A plain-English sorting guide for the paper (and paper-like things) that come with a new job, plus a shredding plan you can finish in one sitting.*

From **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — local-first, no accounts, no data collection. Your papers stay your papers.

---

## The one rule

> If it affects money, identity, or your legal rights — keep it. If it's a duplicate or a "fun free pen" flyer — shred it.

When in doubt: keep it, scanned if possible, until at least the next tax season.

---

## KEEP (originals if you can, scans as backup)

| Paper | Why it matters | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Signed offer letter | Your salary, title, and start date, in writing | Keep while employed + a few years after |
| Signed W-4 (your copy) | Proof of how you set withholding | Current + past 3 years |
| Direct deposit confirmation | Proof of bank details on file | Current + 1 year |
| Form I-9 copy (if they give it) | Work authorization record | Per employer guidance |
| Employee handbook (PDF or paper) | PTO, policies, what you agreed to | While employed + 1 year |
| Benefits enrollment confirmation | Proof of your elections and dates | Coverage year + 2 years |
| Health insurance card & summary of benefits (EOC/SBC) | You need it at every doctor visit | While covered |
| 401(k)/retirement enrollment paperwork | Notes who manages your money and vesting dates | Permanent-ish |
| Pay stubs | Proof of income and deductions | 1 year (or until W-2 matches) |
| W-2 (arrives ~Jan 31 each year) | Needed for taxes | 7 years with your tax return |
| Badge/access agreements or non-disclosure you signed | Legal obligations — know what's in them | Permanent |
| Professional licenses/certifications (nurses, trades, etc.) | Your livelihood, full stop | Permanent |

## SCAN & KEEP (paper original optional)

- Training certificates or "attended" emails
- Parking/transit pass receipts (may be tax-relevant)
- Names & titles of people you met week one (a photo of a business card works)

## SHRED (soon or eventually)

- Extra duplicate copies of forms you already filed
- "Welcome to the gym you didn't sign up for" flyers
- Old visitor passes and event tickets with your name
- Anything showing your **full SSN, DOB, or bank numbers** once you no longer need it — that's shred-now, not shred-someday
- Marketing mail from the company's vendors

**Never trash whole documents with your SSN or bank info in the open recycle bin.** Shred, or black it out and then shred at home if no shredder exists at work.

---

## The 10-minute shredding session

1. [ ] Gather every loose paper from week one into one pile
2. [ ] Sort into three stacks: KEEP / SCAN / SHRED
3. [ ] Photograph each KEEP item (backup copy, in case of fire/flood/lost folder)
4. [ ] File KEEP papers in one folder labeled with the company name + year
5. [ ] Shred the SHRED stack. Done. Reward yourself with something.

- [ ] Shredding session complete (date: ____________)

---

## Where to file (pick one system and stop deliberating)

- A single labeled folder per employer, per year — boring and it works
- A fire/water box if you own originals of permanent items
- A scan-to-cloud or scan-to-USB folder named like `W2_2026.pdf` — searchable future-you says thanks
