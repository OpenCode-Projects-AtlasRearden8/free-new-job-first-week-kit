Papers: Keep or Shred

A 10-minute sorting guide for week-one paperwork  •  employer:   year:
The one rule: if it affects money, identity, or your legal rights — keep it. If it's a duplicate or a flyer — shred it. When in doubt: keep a scan until at least the next tax season.

Keep ✓
