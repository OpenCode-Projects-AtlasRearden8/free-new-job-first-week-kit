# First 90 Days: Reflections One-Pager

*One page, three check-ins (day 30, 60, 90). Twelve five-minute sessions across your first three months — that's the whole program. New-job culture change is real; noticing how it's going keeps small frictions from calcifying into big ones.*

From **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**. ADHD-friendly by design: small check-ins, honest answers, zero "should."

---

## How to use

- At each check-in, answer honestly in short sentences. Bullet fragments are fine.
- These are yours. You don't turn them in. You re-read them at the next check-in and watch the pattern.
- The only rule: don't skip because "nothing to report." There's always something, even if it's "I still don't know where the good stapler lives."

## When something's off: the quiet-first path

1. Re-read your three check-ins — patterns you feel weekly look obvious in writing
2. Try one small, direct fix first (a question, a preference, a boundary said kindly)
3. If a bigger conversation is needed: your manager or HR, with the pattern written down
4. Document facts (dates, what was said) if it's headed toward accountability territory
